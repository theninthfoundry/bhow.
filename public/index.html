STEP 02 / REUNIFICATION PROTOCOL

Verification & Safety

Canine dermatoglyphic patterns on the planum nasale are unique to every dog, similar to human fingerprints.

How Demo Matching Works:
1. Upload or click a test sample.
2. The system computes perceptual visual embeddings.
3. Matches against enrolled dogs (Buddy, Luna, Milo).
4. Returns instant guardian contact details.
Negative Test Verification:
Sample 4 is an unregistered beagle. Scanning it tests rejection accuracy (< 30% confidence) and routes to "Register New Dog".
